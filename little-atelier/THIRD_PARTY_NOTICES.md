# Pequeño Atelier — third-party notices

Original game interface, stamps, backgrounds and illustrations were created for this project. No third-party images, brands, fonts, music or remote assets are bundled.

`core.js` contains a quadratic midpoint brush adapted from `js/board.js` lines 593–602 and 682–686 in [drawingboard.js](https://github.com/manuhabitela/drawingboard.js/tree/92e2cfae33735efdd29885a0f564578055299293), commit `92e2cfae33735efdd29885a0f564578055299293`. The adaptation uses normalized input points, independent state, explicit tap dots and native Canvas instead of jQuery. The remainder of drawingboard.js is not bundled.
