# El jardín de los caminos — third-party notices

Original game interface, rabbit, garden, stars and carrot were created for this project. No third-party images, brands, fonts, music or remote assets are bundled.

`core.js` adapts the stack-based maze traversal and reciprocal wall-removal mechanism in `src/maze.js` lines 86–163 from [maze-generator](https://github.com/keesiemeijer/maze-generator/tree/29da89fd5b43c9e5c521de3ec76d5069d405d76c), commit `29da89fd5b43c9e5c521de3ec76d5069d405d76c`. The adaptation uses seeded randomness, arrays of directional walls, bounded grids and an independent breadth-first hint path. No upstream UI, solver or images are bundled.
